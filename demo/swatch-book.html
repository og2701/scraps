09 · custom colours
Scraps.registerColors({
  rose:  '#C4587A',
  moss:  '#5E7348',
  night: { fill: '#1C2733', text: '#F6F1E4' },
})
registered names become data-color values (and color props in react). the stock palette stays themeable through the --sc-* custom properties.
